# Product integration

## Combined product hierarchy

1. **Organization** — tenant and membership boundary.
2. **Workspace** — the user-facing professional environment.
3. **Engagement/Deal** — a continuing body of evidence, work, decisions and participants.
4. **Workstreams** — scoped areas of responsibility inside an engagement.
5. **Mandates** — flexible AI or human-approved work commissioned against workspace state.
6. **Capabilities** — bounded executable building blocks registered for mandate plans.
7. **Integrity Review** — a capability family that compares evidence and work across the shared state.
8. **Findings/Challenges** — reviewable outputs entering one shared workflow.

The deal is not broader than the workspace. The contradiction engine is not broader than mandates. Integrity Review is powerful because it participates in the whole workflow rather than operating as a separate report generator.

## AI roles

The AI is not limited to “investigation” and “revision.” Mandates may support:

- Investigation and diligence.
- Work-product integrity review.
- Draft or decision-package production.
- Reconciliation and calculation checking.
- Readiness assessment.
- Targeted reassessment after change.
- Opt-in monitoring.

Integrity Review is one family under Mandates. Some Integrity Review runs are explicitly commissioned; later ones may be triggered by an approved event policy, but every run remains visible, scoped, budgeted and auditable.

## Integrity Review definition

Integrity Review asks whether selected statements can safely coexist with selected evidence and peer work.

It may identify:

- Numerical conflicts.
- Period, unit or currency mismatches.
- Calculation or derivation divergence.
- Entity-name or entity-identity conflicts.
- Unsupported factual assertions.
- Management or vendor claims presented as verified fact.
- Superseded or stale support.
- Cross-workstream semantic or logical contradictions.
- Missing or insufficient evidence.

It does not automatically declare which human is wrong. It presents the competing assertions, provenance, uncertainty and recommended resolution path.

## Senior visibility

Deal leads, MDs and authorized executives may inspect:

- Assignments and owners.
- Submission versions and review decisions.
- Evidence and findings.
- Returned and approved work.
- Outstanding requests and blockers.
- Unresolved decision exposure.
- Material changes since the last review.

They must not receive reductive AI employee-quality scores, contradiction leaderboards or inferred performance ratings.

## Positioning

The contradiction capability may be the initial commercial wedge, but the product remains:

> A shared professional workspace where intelligence participates in continuing work, review and decisions.
