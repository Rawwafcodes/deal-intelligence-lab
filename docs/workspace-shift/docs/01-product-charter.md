# Product charter

## Purpose
An AI-native collaborative execution workspace for deal and advisory teams:
analysts do and submit work, AI performs scoped mandates and reviews, and senior
people see evidence, outstanding exposure, responsibilities and decisions.

The initial application is financial diligence, financing and deal review. A broader
workspace hierarchy does not authorize building every professional-service vertical.

## Four connected elements
1. Shared context: workspace, deals, briefs, sources and workstreams.
2. Mandates: durable AI assignments with inspectable plans and governed execution.
3. Team execution: assignments, submissions, comments, requests and review cycles.
4. Senior oversight: current position, material issues, blocked work and drill-down.

## People and visibility
Analysts produce work; reviewers challenge it; deal leads/MDs direct and approve.
Executives see what their access permits. A CEO title is not an access-control rule.
Deal leads see submitted work and shared professional activity, not local keystrokes
or undisclosed drafts outside the application. External executive access is curated.

## Representative journey
An analyst screens an opportunity through a workspace mandate. It becomes a deal
without losing the original work. The lead defines scope and assigns workstreams.
AI proposes and runs a diligence plan over authorized source versions. Findings
become reviewed issues and management requests. Analysts revise submissions.
AI and human review examine those revisions. A lead approves a decision package.
New evidence flags dependent conclusions for reassessment without rewriting history.

## Non-goals now
Office replacement; collaborative spreadsheet editor; VDR connector suite; generic
agent marketplace; arbitrary code on the app host; autonomous investment decisions;
external sending without approval; employee ranking; invented health scores.
Do not advertise support for DOCX/PPTX or other formats merely because upload permits them.

## Success
Users can explain who did the work, which evidence was used, what remains unresolved,
why a decision was approved, and what changed afterward. A second mandate template
using existing capabilities works without a new service-specific page or executor.
